The Tavern

Tools for our D&D 2024 table

Pull up a chair. Everything here runs in your browser and is free to use with your group.

On the menu tonight

Pick a tool. It opens in this tab.

    Where your data is kept

    Without an account, characters and encounters stay in the browser you use. With an account, they're also kept online, and only you can see them. An account is just a username and a password: no email, no real name.

    Changing devices

    Sign in on the other device and everything is there. Without an account, the character sheet's Notes and backup tab saves your character as a file you can load elsewhere or send to your DM.
